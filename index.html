We don't research markets.

We research builders.

The name is intentional. Research — in our world — means signal. Discovery. Early insight.

We surface builders at zero. Before the product launch, before the press release, before the traction. We find the people doing the actual work.

That's the discovery layer. That's Scranton Research.

The Problem

Building is easy now.
Building alone isn't.

AI lowered the floor. Anyone can ship. But most early builders still hit the same three walls.

01

No network

You're building in isolation. You don't know who else is working on the same problems — or who could help you move faster.

02

No feedback

Shipping into the void. Zero users means zero signal. You can't validate what no one has seen.

03

No collaborators

Finding your co-founder, first designer, or first user at zero is nearly impossible through normal channels.

The Solution

A place to connect
before everything else.

Meet other builders

Find people working on ideas at the same stage. No gatekeeping. No traction requirements. Just people who get it.

Find collaborators

Connect with developers, designers, and early founders who are actively looking to build with someone.

Test your ideas

Share what you're working on with people who understand 0→1. Get real feedback — not just likes.

Validate your positioning

Figure out who your product is for before you build everything. Talk to builders who understand the problem space.

What Happens Inside

Real builders.
Real conversations.

Builders share half-finished ideas and get honest input before writing a single line of code.

Developers find co-founders who match their pace, depth, and risk appetite.
