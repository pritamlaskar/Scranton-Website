The Scranton editorial position

Medium's internal algorithm is a black box. You publish, you wait, you hope. Outside traffic is different.

A reader who finds your article from X or from a shared link counts more. Medium pays more for them. Scranton manufactures that outside traffic, systematically, for every writer on our publication.

The Reality

Great writing gets buried inside Medium.

The platform has over 100 million readers. But most of them never see your work. The traffic that pays most comes from outside.

01

Internal traffic pays less

Medium's own distribution counts for less in its partner program than readers arriving from external sources.

02

Your best ideas stay unseen

Every article has angles, facts, or moments that never reach readers outside Medium's walls. That is the leverage being left on the table.

03

Writers cannot do it alone

Running a social amplification layer on top of writing is a full-time job. Most writers cannot sustain both. That is what a publication is for.

Why "unseen" content?

Every long article has a buried gem. A counterintuitive fact, a forgotten story, a line that would stop a scroll. That content is invisible inside Medium's paywall. We pull it out, thread it on the open web, and use it to pull readers into the full article. It is a discovery loop, not a summary.
